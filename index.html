03Master's thesis

A fairness-aware RAG triage assistant

The thesis evaluates the fairness and robustness of an LLM-based decision-support system for clinical triage. A retrieval-augmented pipeline predicts the urgency of a case and the medical specialty, and is compared against plain prompting.

Status
In progress since March 2026
